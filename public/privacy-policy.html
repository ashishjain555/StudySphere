Privacy Policy — StudySphere AI

Last Updated & Effective Date: November 9, 2026 | Application: StudySphere AI (https://studysphere-ai-rktl.onrender.com)

Welcome to StudySphere AI ("we", "us", or "our"). StudySphere AI is an intelligent academic learning, study scheduling, and curriculum platform designed to help students organize their academic journey with artificial intelligence. We are deeply committed to protecting your personal privacy and ensuring total transparency regarding how your data is collected, handled, and safeguarded.

1. Information We Collect

When you use StudySphere AI, we collect minimal information necessary to deliver educational services:

  • Google Account Information (Google Sign-In): When you choose to authenticate via Google, we collect your primary Google email address, full name, and avatar/profile picture. This is retrieved strictly through standard OAuth 2.0 authentication scopes (openid, email, profile).
  • Account Credentials: If you register via email, we securely store your email address and salted hashed password credentials.
  • Academic & Study Data: Study schedules, uploaded course syllabi/notes, generated quizzes, flashcards, and student profile preferences (e.g., semester, major, target learning goals) that you voluntarily enter to enhance your learning experience.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information exclusively for the following educational and functional purposes:

  • To authenticate your identity and maintain a secure student session.
  • To personalize your study dashboard, calendar events, and curriculum progression.
  • To generate custom practice quizzes, summaries, and AI tutor explanations based on your course topics.
  • To ensure system integrity, prevent fraudulent activity, and enforce security policies.
Google User Data Protection Commitment:
StudySphere AI's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

3. Zero Data Sale & Third-Party Sharing

We adhere to strict privacy standards regarding third-party disclosure:

  • No Sale of Personal Data: We NEVER sell, rent, monetize, or trade your personal information or Google account data to data brokers, advertisers, or third parties under any circumstances.
  • No Advertising Networks: We do not serve third-party behavioral advertisements or share your data with ad tracking networks.
  • AI Training Boundaries: Your personal Google account data and private study materials are NOT used to train or fine-tune public foundation AI models.

4. Data Storage & Security Measures

We implement enterprise-grade technical safeguards to keep your data safe:

  • All network communication is strictly encrypted via Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.3 / HTTPS).
  • Database records are protected with PostgreSQL Row Level Security (RLS) policies in Supabase, ensuring that students can strictly access only their own individual study records.
  • Security headers including HTTP Strict Transport Security (HSTS), nosniff, and anti-clickjacking policies are strictly enforced across all application endpoints.

You have full ownership and control over your personal data:
